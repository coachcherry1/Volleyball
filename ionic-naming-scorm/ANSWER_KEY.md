## The rule

**IF** the positive ion is a transition metal → metal (Roman numeral) + negative ion.
**OTHERWISE** → positive ion + negative ion.

Transition metals in this class: Ti, Cr, Mn, Fe, Co, Ni, Cu, Au, Hg, Sn, Pb. Ag and Zn are **not** transition metals (Ag⁺ and Zn²⁺ only).
Mercury is used only as Hg²⁺; mercury(I), Hg₂²⁺, is left out.

## Pairings left out

- Peroxide (O₂²⁻) is paired only with Group 1 and Group 2 metals (not Be). With a transition
  metal its formula collides with an oxide: lead(II) peroxide and lead(IV) oxide would both be PbO₂.
- Ammonium is never paired with nitride, phosphide, oxide or peroxide.
- Chromium is never paired with chromate or dichromate, nor manganese with permanganate.
